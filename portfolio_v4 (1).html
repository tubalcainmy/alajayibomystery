🏨 Research in Progress

GTM in Hospitality

Nigerian Hotels Are Losing
Millions to Broken Booking Systems.

I have spent the past weeks auditing the booking systems of leading Nigerian hotels. What I found is consistent across the industry — and entirely fixable.

💳
No Payment at Booking
Most Nigerian hotel portals collect payment at arrival, not at booking — producing 30–45% higher no-show rates. Every hour between booking and payment is an hour the guest can change their mind.
📅
No Live Room Availability
Several major hotels I audited confirmed bookings for rooms already taken. One property's calendar showed every room fully booked for the next five years — sending all booking traffic to competitors.
🔗
Broken Product Links
Premium room clicks routing to standard room pages. USD pricing with no naira equivalent. Booking forms defaulting to 0 adults. Confirmed revenue leaks on every booking attempt.
💡
The Pricing Opportunity
Restructure pricing so online booking matches the current rate and walk-in carries a 10% premium. Guests believe they saved money. The hotel earns more, earlier, with full commitment upfront.
🛒
No Checkout Upsells
Hotels from ₦80,000 to ₦2,500,000/night capture room revenue only. International properties generate 20–40% incremental revenue from booking-page add-ons — breakfast, early check-in, airport transfers.
🔧
The Fix
Paystack at booking. Live Google Sheets availability. Checkout upsells. Pricing restructure. WhatsApp fallback. GA4 tracking. All on existing infrastructure at zero monthly server cost.
